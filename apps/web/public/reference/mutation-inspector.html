The wild-type residue is checked against the UniProt sequence before anything else — if the notation and the sequence disagree, that gets reported rather than quietly ignored. Then the position is mapped onto a real crystal structure and the local environment is measured: burial, backbone conformation, packing neighbours, hydrogen bonds and salt bridges that the substitution would disturb.

Sequence and curated variants: UniProt REST. Structures: RCSB PDB Search + Data APIs and coordinate files. Rendering: NGL Viewer.
Burial is Shrake–Rupley solvent accessibility computed here from the deposited coordinates; contacts and dihedrals are measured from the same atoms.
