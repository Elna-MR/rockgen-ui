Complementpeptide binder design · mutation sites

Design starts by asking whether the site can be bound at all.

Give a mutation — PFN1-G118V, KRAS G12C, SOD1 A4V — and this measures the surface around it in a real structure, decides whether a peptide could ever reach the mutated side chain, breaks the reachable surface into chemical subsites, then runs simulated annealing over sequence space to build peptides complementary to those subsites.

Every design is scored against the mutant and the wild-type surface, because a binder that cannot tell them apart is not useful.

Sequence: UniProt REST. Structure: RCSB PDB. Accessibility, subsites and contacts are computed here from deposited coordinates. Rendering: NGL Viewer.
This is a hypothesis generator built on geometric and chemical heuristics. It does not compute binding free energy and does not dock.
